These guidelines are not exhaustive, and it is always up to the judgment of Diomedes staff as to what constitutes a fitting restriction/ban

## A. Fairplay

### A.1 - Global Rules
The following rules apply regardless of the context.
- Do not abuse bugs/exploits in Diomedes
  - Ban from accessing Diomedes ranging from 7 days to permanent.
- Do not inject cheats into, patch or modify the Diomedes client
  - Ban from accessing Diomedes permanently.

### A.2 - Private Lobbies
When in a private lobby the rules and mods should be agreed upon by all players involved. However, we (Diomedes) will not enforce player rules, it is up to the organizer(s).

[`A.1 - Global Rules`](#a1-global-rules) and [`B. Player Conduct`](#b-player-conduct) still apply to private lobbies.

### A.3 - Competitive
When playing in the Knockout Cup or Daily Race you must follow these rules
- Do not use the following exploits:
  - Time freeze
  - Speed Glitch
  - Negative weight
  - Weather Rerolling
  - Multi-terminal
  - Cruiser vaulting
  - Gift Box Manipulation
  - Ban from competitive modes ranging from 7 days to permanent.
- Do not use mods that are not included as part of the [competitive approved mod list](https://github.com/DiomedesLC/meta/blob/main/COMPETITIVE_MOD_LIST.md)
  - Ban from competitive modes ranging from 30 days to permanent. 
- Do not modify approved mods
  - This includes patching/modifying as well as using forks (unoffical builds)
  - Ban from competitive modes permanently.

#### A.3.1 - Knockout Cup
- Do not gain information about the current or future days of the quota from outside your point of view
  - Ban from competitive modes ranging from 7 days to permanent.

#### A.3.2 - Daily Race
- External communication (Discord) is allowed

## B. Player Conduct
- Do not use slurs, discriminate, harass or be excessively toxic in game chat.
  - This includes in-game voice as well as text chat
  - Chat mute (Text, Voice or both) ranging from 7 days to permanent.
  - Extreme repeated behaviour may incur restrictions on accessing competitive modes or Diomedes.
- External incidents will be reviewed on a individual basis
